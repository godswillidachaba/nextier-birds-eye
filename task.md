# Prompt

Design and build a premium executive strategy webpage for the IT Department of Nextier that communicates our flagship WIG (Wildly Important Goal) using principles from the 4 Disciplines of Execution (4DX).

The page should look like a modern blend of McKinsey, Palantir, Deloitte, and Stripe design aesthetics: clean, executive, data-driven, premium typography, subtle animations, glassmorphism accents, dark/light mode support, responsive on desktop and mobile, and suitable for presentation to senior leadership and board members.

## Page Objective

Present the vision, execution strategy, roadmap, and expected business impact of the Bird's Eye Platform.

The Bird's Eye Platform is an internally developed enterprise intelligence platform that provides a single source of truth for all Nextier projects, opportunities, bids, revenue, resource utilization, and organizational performance.

The platform will be developed in-house and self-hosted.

A minimum viable product (MVP) will be delivered within 2 months, while the complete enterprise version will be delivered by 31 December 2026.
